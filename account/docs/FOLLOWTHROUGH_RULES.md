# Expected fixture outcomes before correction

Payment delivery history is immutable. Current invoice state is derived from its credit/grant: one invoice can have one credit; a refund remains refunded. A replay of an old event returns its recorded result but cannot change current state. A new delivery for the same refunded payment returns refunded. A new payment attempt on an already-paid invoice is review-only, cannot create debt or another grant, and cannot reactivate auto-renew. A late failed event cannot turn a paid invoice collectible. Paying one invoice never clears another invoice's debt. Cancellation changes auto-renew only; it does not erase collectible debt or paid coverage. Only an accepted new credit, unresolved failure or cancellation with a newer subscription sequence changes subscription state.

A validated full or partial refund observed before credit is durably retained on that immutable payment identity. Later paid/pending/failed deliveries for it remain needs_review, with no new grant, debt or auto-renew activation; cancellation may still disable auto-renew. Full refunds after credit revoke only the linked grant; a partial refund after credit remains manual review without automatic coverage recalculation. No historical ambiguous needs_review event is guessed to be a refund or silently backfilled.

Mixed-mode checks apply at serialized creation and fulfillment. Two competing creation requests admit one mode. If two previously saved opposing invoices are both still open, fulfillment may put the first in review and grant the second after that conflict clears: at most one mode receives a grant; neither order may silently grant both. An active prepaid interval blocks card fulfillment and active card/debt blocks PLS. Events have no independently verified payment-occurrence timestamp: quote expiry is judged at activation after the account lock, with late payments in needs_review.

Usage is 100 admissions per UTC calendar day shared by every account key. Burst is 30 admissions per fixed UTC minute, so adjacent windows can admit 60 across a boundary. Denials roll back both meters. Intervals are half-open [start,end). PLS uses exact seconds; card uses explicit calendar fixtures. Annual/365 choices stay disabled publicly.

Reconciliation retains invalid records, durable attempts and redacted reasons. Permanent validation/ownership/conflict errors go to needs_review; transient failures retry with finite exponential delay and a five-attempt ceiling. One worker processes at most 20 due records/tick. Mail batches contain at most 10 messages; retry is bounded and at least once. Expiry notices concern current continuous coverage, never a disconnected future max(end). Revalidate before SMTP; accepted/in-flight mail cannot be recalled. Shutdown waits for owned in-flight work and skips unsent remainder.

Better Auth owns password/session/token primitives. A consumed or ambiguous-failure link is never reopened; request a fresh link after failure. A bounded fresh-link recovery path must work after an actual SQL-induced internal failure. Timing samples describe only observed HTTP behavior, not cryptographic constant time.
